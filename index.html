🩸 The Blood Dagger

It drinks what it spills. Every 10 throws feeds it enough for one more blacksmith upgrade, in any category.

Unlike every other weapon, it can reach level 4 in damage, durability, stamina cost and reload speed: 16 upgrades for 160 uses. It cannot be upgraded without being used. Max out damage, durability and stamina cost and its true form awakens.

Throw Clash

Pick a mode before the first fight.

Normal: 3 weapon slots, only a Dagger to start — the Blood Dagger drops from the Crimson Baron, real gold costs, fights softened to stay winnable.
Test: 5 pre-upgraded weapons, free test upgrades, cheap costs (current behavior).

Press a weapon (1–5) to throw it on its own arc. Weapons only clash with others on the same arc, so choose an arc to clash or to slip past. When two weapons clash, the one with higher durability wins and loses durability equal to what it broke. Equal durability breaks both. Damage and durability are separate stats: clashes use durability, hits use damage. The boomerang flies the diagonal arc (V) in an oval and only wears down when it hits another boomerang. It can’t be thrown again until it returns, but it’s ready the moment it does, and it takes 5 seconds to recharge if it breaks. R restarts, and H toggles the Scouter’s HP numbers once you have it.
